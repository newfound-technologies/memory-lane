MEMORY LANE  -  quick start (Windows)

1. Double-click "Memory Lane Setup". It installs everything the program needs
   (it downloads what is missing, so keep the computer online for a few minutes
   the first time) and asks whether you want an icon on the desktop.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the file is new and not
   signed: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Open Memory Lane from its icon. It opens in its own window with no black
   box, and closes by itself a few seconds after you close the window.
   (If a task is running, such as organizing, it finishes first.)
(The first time Memory Lane opens it asks where your photos are and where the library
   should go. There is also a User guide page in the program's menu.)
3. Settings: add the folders your photos are in, and a library folder INSIDE
   iCloud Drive (needs "iCloud for Windows"). Choose how files are named.
4. Home: click "Organize everything" (or "Preview first").
5. People: type a name under each face group. Photos: search by name, year,
   month, or what is in the picture.

WHAT "ORGANIZE EVERYTHING" DOES
 - Copies (never moves, edits or deletes) your originals into the library:
   Library\Photos\2019\2019-07 July\..., plus Screenshots, Videos and
   iPhone HEIC folders.
 - Skips exact duplicates; look-alikes go to Library\_Review.
 - Converts iPhone HEIC photos to JPEG (HEIC originals are kept).
 - Smart tags (AI) and Find people (faces). Both run on your computer.
 - Every step can be paused and resumed; re-running skips finished work.

TAGS AND SEARCH: the AI gives each photo up to 3 guesses, but only guesses it is
fairly sure of (or its best one) are shown, so clicking "mountains" now shows
photos that really are mountains. Clicking a tag button filters by that exact tag;
click it again, or "Show all", to clear. The search box still searches words anywhere
(names, dates, folders). Tags you add yourself always count.

FIXING TAGS (Photos page): open a photo and click the x on a wrong tag, or type
a new one. "Select photos" lets you click many photos and add or remove a tag
on all of them. "Manage tags" renames or deletes a tag everywhere. Your
corrections are never overwritten by the AI.

NEW VERSIONS: when you are online, Memory Lane checks for a newer version when it opens and shows a green
bar at the top with a "Download it" button. Click "Not now" to hide it for that version.

AFTER AN AUTOMATIC UPDATE: the next time you open Memory Lane, a window shows only the photos the
weekly run just added. Delete, tag, name people or mark them done right there, then click
"Finished reviewing" (or "Close for now" and it will ask again next time).

VIDEOS: each video shows its first picture; point at it to play a quick preview, click it to
watch with controls. (A few phone videos in an unusual format may only show a grey box.)

NAMED PHOTOS: a green check with the person's name shows on every photo that has been named.
A blue check means the photo is finished without a person: you gave it a tag yourself
or pressed "Mark done". Use the "Finished or not" box next to the search to show only the
photos still not finished.

WORKING ON A GROUP: click a tag button (for example "kids") to see only that group, click
"Select photos", pick the ones that belong together, type a new tag and/or a person, and
click "Apply & finish". Only the selected photos change (the "Replace" tick-box swaps the
old tag for the new one).

SELECTING SEVERAL PHOTOS: click "Select photos", click the photos, type a tag, a new
name ("Rename...") and/or a person's name, then press "Apply & finish" (the Done button
changes to this once you type something). The buttons next to each box apply just that
one change. The bar stays at the top of the window while you scroll.

SCREENSHOTS AND CATEGORIES: on the Photos page, the "All kinds" menu next to the person
menu shows only Screenshots (or Photos, Videos, iPhone HEIC). To sort them: click
"Select photos", pick some, click "Move to folder...", type a folder name (for example
Receipts) and Move. They go to Library/Screenshots/Receipts and also get the tag
"receipts" (untick the box if you only want the folder). Or choose "A folder for each
photo's main tag" to let the AI sort them by what it sees. Tools > "Undo last rename"
puts the files back in their old folders.

If photos are in Screenshots by mistake (family photos, say): show Screenshots, select them,
and click "Move to Photos". They are filed by date with your other photos. Or type a tag
and press "Apply & finish" - while you are looking at Screenshots it will ask whether to
move them too. Undo works for this as well.

RENAMING FILES (Photos page): open a photo, type a new name under the picture
and click "Rename file". To rename many at once (for example several photos
the AI called "mountain" that are really Yellowstone): search or click a tag,
click "Select photos", pick the ones you want, click "Rename...", type the new
name and choose numbering ("Yellowstone 001, 002..." or with the date in front).
Photos stay in their folders and nothing is ever overwritten. Changed your mind?
Tools > "Undo last rename". Your original folders are never touched.

IF "LOOKING FOR FACES" SEEMS STUCK near the end (version 1.8 and earlier): the final
"grouping" step could loop forever when two differently named people looked alike.
Fixed in 1.8.2. Close Memory Lane, start the new version and click
"Find people" (or Organize everything) again. Your scanned faces and names are kept;
it only redoes the quick grouping step.

FIXING A WRONG NAME ON A PERSON: open the photo (Photos page). Under the picture
it says "With: <name> x". Click the x, then type who it really is (or leave it
blank to just remove the name from that photo). If a whole group is wrongly
named, go to People > Named people and click "Not a real name" (the faces go
back to "Who is this?") or "Rename".

PEOPLE YOU DON'T KNOW: you never have to name them. Open the photo and click "Skip (don't name this person)" under their face; Undo brings it back. When you name several selected photos and a photo has more than one face, the name is put on the photo itself instead of guessing which face.

ADD ANOTHER PERSON: open a photo and use "Add another person..." under the faces to mark someone who is in the picture but has no face box (turned away, small, hidden). They then show in searches, the Person list and the People page. The X beside their name removes it.

PLACES: photos taken with location turned on show the nearest town (for example "West Yellowstone, Montana") in the photo view and under the thumbnail, and you can search by it (type "montana" or "paris" in the search box). It is part of "Organize everything", or run it alone from Tools, step 5, "Find places". The town is worked out on this computer from a built-in list; nothing is sent over the internet. "Show on map" is a link that opens the place name in your browser only when you click it. Photos with no location stored in them (screenshots, photos from apps, photos with location turned off) simply have no place. iPhone HEIC photos keep their location when converted to JPEG. The nearest town can be a neighboring one, since the list is towns, not parks or landmarks.

HOME SCREEN COUNTS: click the Photos or Videos number to see them all as small pictures, and keep scrolling to load more. "Small pictures" / "Larger pictures" on the Photos page switches the size any time.

AUTOMATIC WEEKLY RUN: the button for the current state (On or Off) is highlighted green with a check mark, and the status line below is green when on and red when off.

THE KIND LIST (Photos page) only shows kinds that have files in it. When the last Screenshot is moved or deleted, "Screenshots" disappears from the list.

NAME BOXES ARE DROP-DOWNS: wherever you type a person's name (Photos selection bar, a
photo's faces, People > Who is this?) click the small arrow to pick from everyone already
named, in alphabetical order. Clicking into the box selects the old name, so you can just
type over it. A name that is not in the list can simply be typed.

NAMING PEOPLE FROM THE PHOTOS PAGE: to name several at once, click "Select photos",
pick them, type the person's name next to "Name person" and click it. If a photo
has several faces, or you want to pick exactly which face is who, open the photo:
each face is shown under the picture with a box to type its name (clear the box and press Save to remove a name). Photos where no
face could be found get the name as a tag instead, so searching the name still
finds them.

LOOK-ALIKES (new page): photos that look almost the same as one you already have are
set aside in Library/_Review/Possible Duplicates. Open "Look-alikes" in the left menu to
see each one next to the photo it resembles, with size and the higher-resolution one
marked. Choose: delete the set-aside copy, keep both, or keep the set-aside one and
delete the library one. "Do many at once" deletes or keeps them all. Nothing is erased:
deleted photos go to the Trash (Photos > Deleted photos). Your original folders are
never touched.

DELETING FACES/GROUPS (People > Who is this?): hover a face picture and click its x to
remove that one face (a wrong detection or a picture of a picture). "Remove group"
hides the whole group; your photos are not touched. "Delete photos" moves every photo in
the group to the Trash (restore from Photos > Deleted photos).

DELETING PHOTOS (for example, pictures of other pictures): on the Photos page click
"Select photos", click each one to delete (or "Select all shown"), then "Delete".
They are NOT erased: they move to a Trash folder inside your library, and your
original folders are never touched. Click "Deleted photos" to look at them, pick
some and "Restore", or "Empty trash for good" to erase them. Memory Lane will not
bring deleted photos back when you organize again. You can also open one photo
and click "Delete photo". To review a face group before naming it, click
"See photos" under it on the People page, then select and delete.

SMART TAG LABELS (Tools page): the AI chooses from a list of labels. Turn off
one that is often wrong, or add your own (name + what it looks like, e.g.
"yellowstone" / "geysers and hot springs at a national park"). "Redo all"
re-tags every photo with the new list; your own tag corrections are kept.

TOOLS PAGE: each step on its own, plus "Check before you delete" which
confirms every photo in your old folders is safely in the library. It never
deletes anything itself.

AUTOMATIC WEEKLY RUN: Settings > Automatic weekly run. Uses Windows Task
Scheduler. It wakes a sleeping computer, and if the computer was off it catches up
next time you sign in. (A fully shut-down computer cannot wake itself.) A log of automatic runs is
in your user folder: .photokeeper\auto_log.txt

WHERE THINGS LIVE
 - Library folder (iCloud Drive): your photos + a backup of the catalog.
 - The working catalog, thumbnails and face crops are kept on this computer
   (in .photokeeper in your user folder), NOT in iCloud, so sync cannot
   damage them.

IF SOMETHING SAYS "MISSING": the Home page shows a yellow notice naming the add-on
that did not install. Run "Memory Lane Setup" again (it repairs missing add-ons).
Messages are saved in the file memorylane.log inside the .photokeeper folder in
your user folder; send that file if you need help.

UPDATING: run the new "Memory Lane Setup" over the old one. Your photos, library,
names and tags are kept (they live in the .photokeeper folder and your library folder).
If you turned on the weekly automatic run, open Settings and click "Turn on" once more
after an update so it points at the new copy.

REMOVING: Windows Settings > Apps > Memory Lane > Uninstall. Your photos and library
folder are never deleted.

IF YOU USED AN EARLIER VERSION: delete the old test "Photo Library" folder
and run again. Your originals were never touched.
